## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Develop a smart digital platform that directly connects farmers with consumers, retailers, supermarkets, and hotels, eliminating unnecessary intermediaries. Farmers can list their agricultural products, set fair prices, and receive direct payments. The platform will provide real-time market price updates, AI-based price recommendations, and delivery support. This will help farmers increase their income, reduce consumer prices, minimize food wastage, and create a transparent agricultural marketplace.

## Technical Approach
1. Frontend Development: Build a user-friendly mobile application and website using HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React.

2. Backend Development: Use Python (Django/Flask) or Node.js to manage users, products, orders and transactions.

3. Database Management: Use MySQL or MongoDB to store farmer details, product information, prices and customer orders.

4. AI Integration: Implement machine learning to analyze market demand, supply and historical prices to recommend fair selling prices.

5. Real-Time Market Updates: Integrate market price APIs to provide updated agricultural price information.

6. Payment Integration: Enable secure digital payments through UPI and other payment gateways.

7. Logistics Management: Connect farmers with transport services to ensure timely delivery of agricultural products.

8. Multilingual Support: Provide regional language options to make the platform accessible to farmers.

Overall Workflow: Farmer Registration → Product Listing → Price Recommendation → Direct Customer Connection → Order Placement → Secure Payment → Product Delivery.

Research:

The proposed solution is based on research into agricultural marketing problems, farmers' income and digital marketplaces.
- Research shows that farmers often receive a limited share of the final consumer price due to marketing costs, intermediaries and transportation expenses.
- The Government of India introduced e-NAM (National Agriculture Market) in 2016 to improve transparent agricultural trading and connect farmers with buyers.
- Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) help farmers work collectively, improve bargaining power and access larger markets.
- Digital platforms can provide real-time market prices, online trading and direct payment facilities.
- AI-based price recommendations can help farmers make informed selling decisions.
